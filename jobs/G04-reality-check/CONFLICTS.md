# Selected conventions

Reality Check is an original job specification,not a claim to reproduce an
existing publisher's official game. Bluff analogues specify four/seven rounds;
select the job's eight,with a doubled final round. Their truth/fooled-vote
ratio motivates1000/500 point units; duplicate fake answers will share fooled
vote credit and self-votes will be invalid. Exact-correct written answers get
truth credit once and do not also vote. Log(1+x) metrics handle zeros but lose
exact scale invariance; select the derived ratio rule with explicit zero cases.
These are design decisions to be finalized and tested,not verified universal
rules about the cited games.
