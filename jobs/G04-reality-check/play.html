How the scoring works

Numbers score up to 1,000 for multiplicative closeness: half or twice the answer earns 500. Left/right is exact. Dates lose 250 per century or decade away, down to zero. BCE meets CE with no century zero.

Bluffs earn 1,000 for finding truth and 500 for each fooled voter. Duplicate authors share those 500 points, rounded down. You cannot vote for your own bluff. Writing the truth earns its credit once. Missing answers score zero.

The wheel spins for 3 seconds. New realms teach an unscored 10-second demo. Estimates have 50 seconds; writing has 40; voting has 30; reveals last 15. Submitting everyone’s answer advances early. The host can pause, move on or end.
