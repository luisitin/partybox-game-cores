# Reality Check selected rules

1. Play2–8 seats over eight rounds. Choose Quick only,Mixed(default),Bluff only.
2. The seeded wheel chooses a realm each round. Its first appearance gives a
   10-second public demo with a different example,then the scored question.
3. Quick types: number estimate,left/right choice,century slider,decade dial.
   Positive numeric score is1000*min(guess,truth)/max(guess,truth),rounded;
   reciprocal errors are equal. Equal zeros score1000,one zero scores0.
   Choices are exact; date proximity will use an explicit bounded rule.
4. Everyone writes a believable fake on Bluff rounds. Truth and distinct
   normalized fake answers appear in seeded anonymous order. Vote for truth;
   own fakes cannot be chosen. Find truth for1000; fooled votes earn500,
   shared among duplicate authors. Accidental correct writes earn truth
   credit once and do not vote. Missing submissions/votes scorezero.
5. The final round doubles all awards. Highest total wins; top ties shared.
6. Deadlines advance every phase. VIP pause/resume/skip/end are supported;
   stale/early timers,spectators,wrong-phase actions and paused inputs ignored.
7. Private answers/authors remain hidden until their public reveal. A local
   hot-seat page must conceal each controller until its owner reveals it.

This is the selected original rulebook; cited genre conventions and alternatives
are in SOURCES.md/CONFLICTS.md. Precise timing/date rules remain in progress.
