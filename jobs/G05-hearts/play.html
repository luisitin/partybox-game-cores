House rules & seed

A quick guide to the table

  1. Each heart is +1; Q♠ is +13. Lowest total wins when someone reaches the selected finish score.
  2. Select three cards on passing hands. Everyone receives together. Pass left/right/hold at3 seats; left/right/across/hold at4; left/right/second-left/second-right/hold at5; add opposite before hold at6.
  3. The lowest club remaining opens: 2♣ normally,3♣ if2♣ was removed,4♣ at6 seats. Follow the led suit whenever possible; only the highest card in that suit wins.
  4. Avoid hearts and Q♠ on the first trick unless you have only point cards. Hearts cannot be led before being broken unless you hold only hearts.
  5. Take all thirteen hearts and Q♠ to shoot the moon. The chosen moon rule applies; an enabled J♦ bonus goes to its actual taker separately.

Even deals: at3 seats remove2♦ (or2♣); at5 remove2♣/2♦; at6 remove2♣/3♣/2♦/2♠. Human tables wait for Continue after a trick or scored hand. Hide the hand before handing the screen over.
