# Actual disagreements and selections

- Pagat/Bicycle use 20/10 Gin/undercut; Neller and CardGames.io use 25/25.
  Both presets are exposed; neither is labelled the single universal official rule.
- Bicycle's prose suggests layoffs after Gin. Pagat, Neller, CardGames.io
  and Wikipedia explicitly forbid them. Use the explicit prohibition.
- Dealer: winner (Pagat/Bicycle), alternating (Neller/Wikipedia), loser (variants).
  All three are settings; rotation uses the documented house queue convention.
- Rubl doubles the whole score in a shutout; Pagat classic doubles only the
  game bonus; CardGames.io doubles hand scores and excludes drawn hands.
  Presets expose scoring formulas, with drawn hands not preventing a shutout.
- RuleBook explicitly credits Wikipedia; Tally & Table attributes its Big Gin
  claim to Wikipedia. Neither counts as an independent second Big Gin source.
  CardGames.io's independently described implemented rules and W support 31.
- Cool Old Games includes a ten-point successful-knock bonus and ambiguous
  Gin doubling prose. Do not adopt those; Pagat's exact differential and
  whole-hand spade formula are selected and tested. O supports the separately
  written limit/ace/spade variant facts, not those disputed score formulas.
- Standard stock ends at two after discard; no tournament last-chance exception.
- Wikipedia mentions first-turn knock as misdeal; P explicitly permits it.
  Pick P, also consistent with the first-turn flow in Neller/C.
- Four-player winner-stays is an owner-requested house extension, not an
  invented official four-player rule. Pagat's official four-player partnership
  variant is documented but not mislabeled as the requested rotation.
