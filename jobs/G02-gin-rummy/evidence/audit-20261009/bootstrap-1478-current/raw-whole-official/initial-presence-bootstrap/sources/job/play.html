Rules and scoring

Deal ten. Accept or pass the first upcard; two passes force a stock draw. Then draw and discard. Sets need three or four matching ranks; runs need three consecutive cards of one suit. Aces are low.

Knock with at most ten deadwood (or the Oklahoma upcard limit). Gin has no deadwood. Big Gin melds all eleven cards without discarding. Defenders may lay off after a normal knock, never after Gin. Equal deadwood undercuts. Two stock cards after a discard draw the hand.

Hand points reach the target before game and line bonuses are added. Classic bonuses: Gin 20, undercut 10, each won hand 20. North American: 25/25/25. Rubl: 25/20/25. Big Gin: 31 or the explicit 50-point house rule. Oklahoma can double spade hands.

Three/four players use the requested house rotation: winner stays, oldest waiting player joins, loser moves to the back. This is not partnership Gin.
