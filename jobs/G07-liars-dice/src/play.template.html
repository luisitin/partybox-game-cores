How to play & special rounds

Raise or call. A bid says how many dice of one face are under all cups. Increase the quantity, or keep the quantity and increase the face. With wild ones, ones help faces 2–6. To bid ones from another face, use at least half the old quantity, rounded up. From ones to another face, use at least twice the quantity plus one.

Dudo & calza. Dudo challenges the last bid: if enough matching dice exist, the caller loses a die; otherwise the bidder does. Optional calza calls an exact count: right gains one die (up to five), wrong loses one. Calza is unavailable with two players left or during palifico. The selected calza policy controls who may call.

Palifico. When enabled, a player starts palifico once, after their first loss from two dice to one and with at least three players remaining. There is none with two players left. Ones are not wild. The opening face stays locked by default. Both face-change exceptions require having started your own palifico before. Experienced one-die players also need exactly one die now; Any experienced player may have any remaining dice count. The current starter is never exempt.

Keep your cup private. Switch the device holder to make an optional calza call from another seat. Cups close on every move, handoff, pause, and new round. A turn clock, when enabled, keeps running while cups are covered. Seven and eight seats extend the usual table size.
