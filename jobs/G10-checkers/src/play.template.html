A quick guide to the rules

American

Men move and capture forward. Kings move one diagonal square. Choose any available capture sequence and finish its jumps. Reaching the crown row ends that turn. Forty moves by each side without a capture or man move is a draw.

International

Men move forward and capture in either direction. Kings travel diagonally across empty squares. Take the greatest possible number of pieces; jumped pieces stay as blockers until the move finishes. A man becomes a king only when the sequence ends on the crown row. Official draws include 25 moves each and shorter king endings.

Threefold repetition counts the same board with the same side to move. The optional turn clock awards the game to the other side when time runs out. Selecting 40 moves each replaces the official move-count rules with that house rule.
